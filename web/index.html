    Research methods

    This public version supports chat and registered NHIS analyses. Data preparation and independent model benchmarks are managed by the site operator. Cookies keep this browser’s chat and analysis history separate from other visitors.

    The registered NHIS analyses cover U.S. adults aged 25–64 in 2024. Outcomes include fair or poor self-rated health, insurance coverage or uninsured status at interview, and medical care delayed or needed but not obtained because of cost during the past 12 months. Each item uses its own registered valid-response denominator. Insurance coverage and a negative cost-related response do not establish that all care was obtained promptly.

    Respect the survey design

    Construct the survey design on the full adult sample before subsetting. Use WTFA_A weights, PSTRAT strata, and PPSU sampling units to report estimates, standard errors, and 95% confidence intervals.

    Income and missing data

    Income analyses pool all 10 official imputations using Rubin rules. Incomplete inputs block calculation.

    Release checks and limitations

    Cells that fail sample-size or reliability checks are suppressed. Gradients are risk differences; regressions report adjusted and unadjusted odds ratios. Descriptive associations are not causal effects or individual medical advice.

    R/Stata agreement and comparison with external NCHS benchmarks remain pending independent verification. Model responses are measured separately and cannot replace the survey.
